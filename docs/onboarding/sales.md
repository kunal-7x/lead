# Sales Team Onboarding

Daily workflow:

1. Open the dashboard inbox.
2. Prioritize hot leads and human handoffs.
3. Review each lead detail page before calling.
4. Confirm site visits and update attendance.
5. Use WhatsApp threads for brochure, reminder, and follow-up messages.
6. Keep SLA: hot handoff within 5 minutes, site-visit confirmation same day.
7. Mark wrong number, opt-out, broker, fake, or not interested immediately.

AI summaries are assistance only; verify legal, pricing, possession, and loan claims with approved project material.
