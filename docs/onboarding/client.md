# Client Owner Onboarding

Day 1 workflow:

1. Create tenant and verify billing account.
2. Invite sales managers and salespeople.
3. Add a project and upload KB documents.
4. Wait for KB approval and RERA checks.
5. Import leads or connect lead sources.
6. Define campaign script, caps, calling windows, and WhatsApp templates.
7. Launch a small pilot campaign.
8. Review dashboard reports, site visits, handoffs, and billing usage.

Demo mode is available for training without real calls or WhatsApp sends.
